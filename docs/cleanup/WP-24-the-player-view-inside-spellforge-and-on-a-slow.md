# WP-24 · The Player View inside Spellforge and on a slow phone

Part of the [Atlas cleanup list](README.md) — **second pass** (2026-09-26).

**Goal:** Players reach the map through Spellforge's Map tab, often on phones. The framed map should not trap the pointer, the Back button or page scrolling, should keep the player's place across tab switches, and should load and navigate without extra round trips.

**Do after:** [WP-09](WP-09-the-player-view-on-phones.md), [WP-23](WP-23-the-player-view-shows-the-right-map-moment-art-a.md)

**Notes:** P103 and the main half of P105 are fixes in ~/repos/spellforge: keep MapTab mounted and hide it, and size the tab with flex instead of calc(100dvh - N); P103 needs nothing on the Atlas side. B107 is one prop (onLostPointerCapture) in MapPlane — do not remove the Origin-Agent-Cluster header to work around it. B108 passes {replace:true} to PlayerView's navigate calls, and only when framed. C095 replaces the string replace on helmet's CSP with a /p-only CSP and an EMBED_ORIGINS setting, and adds a header test; its CLAUDE.md line overlaps C103 in WP-27. P106 lazy-loads the DM pages so /p stops downloading the Forge and the Dashboard. P107 fetches /world and the map in parallel, keeping C002's split between world-404 and map-404. P104 replaces partyTrail's per-map walkUp loop, whose cost grows with every map the Party visits; B010's reachableNode should share that memo. Borders WP-09; kept separate because the first pass never ran the Player View inside a frame.

### P104 · Party trail share payload runs one walkUp per visited map on every Player View fetch and poll, adding about 230 ms at 15 maps and growing with the campaign

- **Where:** server/routes/share.js:238-257 (partyTrail loop; per-map walkUp at :246, cache keyed by map only), walkUp at :47-67; client/src/pages/PlayerView.jsx:47-68 (load() always windowed; runs on mount, mapId change, 45 s interval, visibilitychange)
- **What happens:** The Party visited 15 distinct maps: the root, 5 towns, 5 buildings and 4 cellars, up to 3 levels deep. walkUp costs 1 + 3×depth sequential queries per map, so each windowed map request makes 96 extra sequential DB round trips. Medians of 3 live calls: root windowed 338 ms vs plain 112 ms; town windowed 350 ms; cellar windowed 373 ms vs plain 150 ms. That is about 2.4 ms per walkUp query. Longer term, compute partyTrail once per (world, canon) and serve it from /:token/world, which PlayerView already fetches on every load, instead of rebuilding it inside every map payload.

### P107 · Every Player View navigation and poll fetches /world first and only then the map

Product polish · low · effort xs · second pass, found by `big-world`

- **Where:** client/src/pages/PlayerView.jsx:47-60 (load: getWorld().then(() => getMap(...))), used on mount, on every mapId change and by the 45 s poll and visibilitychange at :63-68
- **What happens:** load() chains the two requests, so every interior entered, crumb tapped or ⬆ back waits for a full /world round trip before the map request even starts. /world returns name, eras and canon, which don't change between maps. On slow 4G the cold load's /world took 275 ms and the windowed map 574 ms. A later refresh was /world at 160 ms, then the map at 383 ms. With the map's own ~350 ms of server time on a long campaign (see the partyTrail finding), a tap into a building is about 0.5–0.7 s before anything draws.
- **Why it matters:** When the URL already names the map, the map request goes out immediately, in parallel with /world (or /world is refetched only on the poll).
- **Fix:** In load(), when mapId is set, request both at once with Promise.all([getWorld(token), getMap(token, mapId, viewTRef.current, true)]). Only the bare /p/<token> case should wait for /world to learn rootMapId. Handle the two rejections separately, so C002's distinction between a dead link (world 404) and an unavailable place (map 404) still works. On navigation, you can also reuse the world already in state and refresh it only on the poll and on visibilitychange. Locally at 150 ms RTT this saves one round trip (about 175 ms) on each tap into an interior, crumb, back or locate.
