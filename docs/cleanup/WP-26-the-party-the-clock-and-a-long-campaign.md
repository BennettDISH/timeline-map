### C099 · Footprint trail at campaign length: the dotted path joins visits sessions apart with long straight lines, and the 8 session colours repeat

Confusing · low · effort s · second pass, found by `big-world`

- **Where:** client/src/components/PartyTrail.jsx:11-13, 36-42 (one polyline through every print on the map in time order) and :45-50 (print colour); client/src/utils/moment.js:4-6 (SESSION_COLORS, 8 hues cycled by idx % 8). - **What happens:** On the root (the party was there in sessions 1, 2, 8–10, 16–18, 24–26, 32–34 and 40) the one dotted polyline ran through all 74 prints. Where the party left the map for five sessions and came back, it drew a straight line between the exit and re-entry points, as if they had walked it. 3 such segments were longer than 10% of the map, the longest 38%.
